About

I'm the sole IT engineer at a power station, which means the job has no edges: one day it's an approval engine in a Flask app, the next it's redesigning the core switch topology, extracting settlement data from the electricity market, or standing up a new internal service from scratch.

Alongside that, I run King Bytes, a small software studio in the Philippines. It's where the systems I've proven internally grow into commercial products other businesses can use. I like building things that are honest about what they do, easy to hand over, and still standing months later.
